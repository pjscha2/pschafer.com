The system

Value flows to whichever physical layer is the binding constraint. The map holds the candidate layers, including ones not yet named. Where and how hard the constraint binds is read from filings each quarter, never assumed — and when innovation routes around a constraint, the map is built to show it relaxing.

The constraint map

Rows are physical layers; columns are the five tells a filing can show. A cell is PRESENT only when found in a filing. Where more than one company has been read on a layer, the cell shows how many of them show the tell — "2 of 3" — never the union. Evidence chips: [F] filing-grade; [F-KR/PROV] a provisional Korean pattern set that a Korean-reading reviewer may overturn. Hover a cell for the names.

The five tells, defined

signatureplain Englishwhat it looks like in a filingstrength
prepaymentsCustomers pay before delivery.Customer deposits or contract liabilities growing faster than revenue; "advance payments from customers".Strong — cash moves before product does.
capacity_reservationCustomers book future output in advance."capacity reservation agreement", "long-term supply agreement" with committed volumes or reserved wafer starts.Strong — commitment to a specific supplier.
lead_timeOrder-to-ship time is stretching.MD&A language: "supply constrained", "extended lead times", "allocation".Weakest of the five — can be inherited from upstream, not owned.
take_or_payCustomer pays whether or not it takes the product."take-or-pay", "minimum purchase commitments", "non-cancellable".Strongest — the buyer has accepted the supplier's terms.
gm_pricing_powerMargin rises on the same product.Gross margin expanding without a mix shift; price increases disclosed in MD&A.Strong but lagging — shows after the others.
