Glossary hooks

substrate (crystal wafer)

the semiconductor wafer a chip is grown or cut from: InP (indium phosphide, for lasers and photodiodes), Si (silicon), GaAs, SiC; on the map, rung `substrate` — the crystal, not the package.

package substrate

the laminate (ABF-based, Ibiden / Unimicron class) a finished die is mounted on inside its package; belongs to `advanced_packaging`, never to the `substrate` rung.

interposer

a silicon or organic bridge layer between dies and the package substrate that carries the dense wiring (HBM ↔ compute); a packaging part.

CoWoS

TSMC's Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate 2.5D packaging that stacks logic and HBM on an interposer; the packaging rung's named capacity constraint.

OSAT

outsourced semiconductor assembly and test: the contract packagers (Amkor, ASE) that assemble and test dies for others; expressers on `advanced_packaging`.

EML

electro-absorption modulated laser: the InP laser-plus-modulator chip inside high-speed transceivers; the optical rung's most constrained component.

CPO

co-packaged optics: optical engines mounted on the switch or accelerator package instead of in pluggable transceivers; a route-around candidate for pluggables, timed on the consensus record (`core/consensus-timestamps.json`).

SiPh

silicon photonics: optical devices (modulators, waveguides) fabricated on silicon wafers in a foundry; the transceiver layer beneath CPO and pluggables.

HBM

high-bandwidth memory: DRAM stacks bonded beside the compute die; rung `memory_bandwidth`.

take-or-pay

a contract in which the buyer pays for a minimum volume whether or not it takes delivery; one of the five tells, read on the seller's side only.

RPO

remaining performance obligations: revenue contracted but not yet recognised; an instrument beside the tells, never a tell.

contract liabilities

cash a customer has paid the seller ahead of delivery (deposits, advances, deferred revenue); an instrument — a tell only when the filer's words say who paid and why.

prepayments

customers pay the seller in advance, in the seller's own words (deposits, advances, down payments).

capacity_reservation

customers pay or commit to reserve the seller's capacity (a slot, a line, a wafer volume).

lead_time

the seller states that demand exceeds its supply, or allocates product.

take_or_pay

a binding minimum-volume commitment the seller discloses.

gm_pricing_power

the seller attributes gross margin to price (not to mix, volume or cost).

PRESENT / ABSENT / NOT_ASSESSABLE / UNKNOWN

the four cell states; a pattern hit is not PRESENT and a miss is not ABSENT — a hand read decides.

WEAK

present, but on language the filer hedges or a caption without an operative sentence.

provisional

a PRESENT sourced from a press release or a provisional pattern set (`R-PR-PROVISIONAL`); counts toward "any", never toward the badge intersection, and 0 in the bottleneck factor.

HOLD

a cell kept as recorded but not counted until the filing's own financials carry it.

badge

a rung's headline "k of 5 · all N names": cells PRESENT for every counted expresser; "any" is the union beneath it.

[F]

a filing: 10-K, 10-Q, 20-F, 6-K, 8-K, or an XBRL fact from one; the highest grade.

[A]

an article or a voice's post: a claim someone else made; never a cell.

[PR]

a company's own press release or published page; provisional until a filing carries it.

[D]

a dataset or a public record (a regulator's docket, a data provider's series).

[P]

the principal's own capture or hand read (a screenshot, a listen, a note).

[I]

an inference the house states and dates (an estimated filing date, a falsifier trigger).

[C]

a figure computed from tagged inputs (a ratio, a share, a cash residual).

[WV]

a page fetched and pinned by the house with URL, SHA-256 and timestamp.
